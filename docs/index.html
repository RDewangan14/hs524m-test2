Where the surface expanded

Analysis of Raipur Municipal Corporation territory (173.64 km²) using Sentinel-2 L2A derived Dynamic World (10 m) land cover across an 8-year post-monsoon baseline.

Coverage result. Total built-up surface increased from 39.98% (69.42 km²) to 48.85% (84.82 km²) of the municipal territory between 2018 and 2025.
Southeastern growth corridor. The most prominent conversion corridor links Old Raipur with Atal Nagar (Naya Raipur) along VIP Road and the Raipur–Abhanpur highway, driving rapid conversion of agricultural and peri-urban land into residential gated communities and logistics hubs.
Western industrial axis. Substantial built densification concentrated along National Highway 53 (Great Eastern Road) through Tatibandh and Sarona toward Durg-Bhilai.
Dynamic Temporal Output

Eight years in eight frames

Annual evolution of built-up density across Raipur Municipal Corporation from 2018 through 2025, using a consistent post-monsoon (October 15 – December 15) observation window.

Temporal progression. Built-up surface expanded consistently in every consecutive annual interval, rising from 69.42 km² in 2018 to 84.82 km² in 2025.
Tree canopy dynamics. Tree cover contracted from 29.85 km² (17.19%) to 25.62 km² (14.75%). Tree loss was concentrated along road-widening corridors and edge subdivisions, while mature canopies inside AIIMS, NIT, Raj Bhavan / Civil Lines, and the Telibandha Lake precinct remained highly resilient.
Built-to-tree ratio. The ratio of built-up land to tree canopy shifted upwards from 2.33 : 1 in 2018 to 3.31 : 1 in 2025 (+42.1% relative increase in urban dominance).

Spatial Interpretation

Spatial pattern and interpretation

Urban morphology and environmental trade-offs during Raipur's transition from regional center to administrative and economic capital.

Asymmetric Growth Vectors: Unlike concentric cities, Raipur's outward physical footprint has been heavily guided by planned infrastructure corridors. The master-planned connection to Atal Nagar (Naya Raipur) in the southeast acts as the dominant magnet for private gated developments, educational campuses, and commercial complexes.
Industrial Infill & Northern Sprawl: To the north, the boundary adjoining Birgaon and Bhanpuri witnessed extensive transformation of scrubland into heavy transport hubs, iron/steel processing yards, and storage warehouses.
Institutional Green Anchors: While open peri-urban land was consumed rapidly, Raipur's core urban canopy was sheltered by large institutional green anchors (AIIMS, NIT, Raj Bhavan, Pt. Ravishankar Shukla University) and water preservation precincts around Telibandha Lake and Budha Talab.
Methodology & Critical Appraisal

Method, sources and limitations

Data lineage, cloud-masking protocol, and technical constraints governing Sentinel-2 and Dynamic World.

Boundary Lineage

City boundary derived from official Indian Urban Local Body boundaries (ramSeraph/indian_admin_boundaries, SBM_ULBs dataset, ULB Code: 802034, total area 173.64 km²).

Seasonal Window Protocol

To eliminate cloud obscuration from the Indian summer monsoon (June–September) and avoid pre-monsoon deciduous leaf-off drying (April–May), a consistent October 15 – December 15 post-monsoon window was applied across all eight years.

10 m Spatial Resolution

While 10 m is substantially finer than 30 m Landsat, small urban elements like individual street trees or small rooftop patches fall below the 100 m² pixel footprint, resulting in mixed-pixel spectral blending.

Deep Learning Class Ambiguity

Dynamic World employs a deep CNN predicting multi-class probabilities. Early-stage construction excavations produce bare earth that can exhibit transient confusion with natural bare soil before structural surfaces emerge.
